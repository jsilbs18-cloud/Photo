**Important sourcing caveats** (carried onto the deck's caveats slide):

- **Primary-site access.** In this environment `census.gov`, `imf.org`, and numerous national statistics and government sites returned **HTTP 403** to automated fetching. Figures were therefore taken from Census/IMF-derived channels — **USTR country trade fact sheets** and the **Census/UN-COMTRADE series mirrored by Trading Economics** for U.S. bilateral goods trade, and **StatisticsTimes' tabulation of IMF WEO (October 2025)** for GDP. These are faithful to the underlying official data but were not read directly off the primary agency page.
- **Currency conversion.** Several overall-trade-balance figures are **converted from local currency** (EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, SAR) at approximate 2025 average exchange rates; the originating office publishes only in local currency. Exact USD values carry a few percent of exchange-rate uncertainty. Local-currency originals are given in each country's notes.
- **GDP confidence.** 13 of 19 GDP values come from the single consistent IMF-WEO-Oct-2025 tabulation (high confidence). **Six** — Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Mexico, Poland, Saudi Arabia — could not be pinned to that exact table and were supplemented from other IMF-based aggregators (may reflect the later April 2026 WEO revision); these are flagged.
- **Basis consistency.** U.S. bilateral figures and most overall balances are **goods-only**. Exceptions are flagged inline (Australia is goods+services; India is fiscal year 2025-26).
